The Story

Built From Layer Zero Built From Layer ZerO

Layer Zero is the foundation beneath every system. It is the material, hardware, and physical structure that makes everything above it possible.

Layer Zero Labs Layer ZerO Labs applies that philosophy to manufacturing: thoughtful design, controlled processes, and dependable physical results.

Founded in Georgia by a systems builder, LZL helps customers move from an existing model, reference object, sketch, or early idea to a finished FDM part with practical technical guidance throughout.

  • Open and enclosed FDM capacity — matched to part and material requirements
  • Multi-color production — color-mapped parts, labels, and display pieces
  • Engineering and flexible materials — selected according to the use case
  • CAD and repair workflows — modeling, reverse design, and print optimization
  • Substrate — our in-house production and slicing workspace
  • Finishing and assembly — sand, prime, paint, install hardware, and assemble
  • Georgia-based — local pickup + nationwide shipping
